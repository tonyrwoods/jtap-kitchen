## App Store Description

**JTAP Kitchen — Where Every Bite Tells a Story**

Experience Memphis's premier high-end casual dining destination right from your pocket. JTAP Kitchen brings bold flavors, warm hospitality, and real culinary artistry together in one beautifully crafted app.

### 🍽️ Reserve Your Table
Book a table in seconds with our intuitive reservation system. Browse availability, choose your date and party size, and get instant confirmation — all without picking up the phone.

### 📋 Explore the Menu
Discover our seasonal, farm-to-table menu with stunning food photography. Filter by category, see dietary tags at a glance, and find chef's picks and featured dishes curated just for you.

### 🎉 Host Unforgettable Events
Planning a birthday, corporate gathering, or celebration? Explore our Event Center packages — from Social Gatherings to Full Buyouts — and submit inquiries directly through the app.

### 🏆 Tap Room Society & Loyalty Rewards
Join the Tap Room Society, earn points on every visit, climb membership tiers, and unlock exclusive rewards. Founding Members get their name on the wall — literally.

### 🎁 Gift Cards
Send digital gift cards to friends and family instantly. Perfect for any occasion — birthdays, anniversaries, or just because.

### 💬 AI Dining Assistant
Chat with our intelligent dining assistant to get instant answers about reservations, menu recommendations, dietary accommodations, and more.

### 🔔 Stay Connected
- Real-time waitlist notifications when your table is ready
- Reservation reminders so you never miss a booking
- Newsletter signup for seasonal updates and exclusive events
- Push notifications for special offers and announcements
